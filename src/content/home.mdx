# Hello!

Welcome to Book & Table. We believe literature is life, good food is magic, travel is transformative and conversations are the sparks that build friendships. We started Book & Table because we want a world where people take the time to explore, read books, gather together, share food and talk about things that matter.

Our gatherings, supper clubs and retreats invite people to get curious, talk about what moves them, and take a beat from the whirlwind that is everyday life to rest, read and reflect. So take a seat at our table—join us for a night out dedicated to reading, come to a supper club and leave with a long list of books to add to your TBR pile and new bookish friends in your phone, or press pause on your daily circus at one of our reading and cooking retreats.

We are so glad you are here and can’t wait to find out what you bring to the table.
