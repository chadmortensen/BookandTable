## A shared love of the good stuff

Book & Table began with a simple idea: life feels richer when we make time for a beautiful meal, a memorable book and an honest conversation.

We’re Meredith and Willi, and we’re creating the kinds of gatherings we always want to attend—warm, curious and unhurried. This is where we’ll share more of our story, the books that shaped us and what we hope to build together.

> “There is no surer foundation for a beautiful friendship than a mutual taste in literature.”
