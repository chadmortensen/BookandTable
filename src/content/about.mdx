## A friendship grown around the table

We are Meredith Mortensen and Willi Galloway. Book & Table is really a story of our friendship, which began with a series of wandering conversations about books, travel, and recipes as we pulled weeds together in our shared vegetable garden in Portland, Oregon. Meredith is a pastry chef and the founder of Cookshop—a cooking school—and Willi is a small business owner, garden writer and recipe developer who teaches gardening classes at Cookshop.

We both love books, food and throwing a really good party—and wanted to invite more people to our table. So we’ve launched Book & Table with the idea of building a community centered on books, conversation and food. Like any good recipe it’s sure to evolve as we sprinkle in more events, collaborate with more people, and read more books.

> We can’t wait to meet you in real life.

In the meantime, you can follow our adventures on Instagram and Substack.
