## Come gather around the table

Our calendar will include reading nights, seasonal supper clubs and restorative retreats built around books, food and place.

### Reading nights

An evening to put away your phone, settle in with a book and enjoy the quiet company of fellow readers.

### Supper clubs

A generous meal and a lively conversation, inspired by a story, a place or an idea worth lingering over.

### Retreats

A longer pause from the whirlwind—time to read, cook, explore and return home restored.
